Our Services

We offer four distinct service tracks. Eligibility, duration, and intensity vary by track. Our online intake form asks a few branching questions to route you to the tracks that fit your household.

Individual Support →

Single-adult case management, benefits enrollment, and mental health referrals.

Family Services →

Whole-family support with age-appropriate services for each child.

Emergency Response →

24-hour crisis intake for DV survivors and families in imminent crisis.

Long-Term Recovery →

18-month intensive coaching toward permanent housing and stable income.
