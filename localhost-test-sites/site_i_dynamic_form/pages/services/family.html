Family Services

Comprehensive support for families with children under 18.

Our Family Services track supports both parents and children in the household. Adults receive standard case management; children are matched with age-appropriate services such as after-school tutoring, school-supply assistance, and youth mentorship groups. Grandparent-headed households and kinship placements are served through the same track.

What's Included for Parents/Guardians

  • Weekly case management
  • Parenting classes (Nurturing Parenting Program, evidence-based)
  • Financial coaching and emergency assistance
  • Legal referrals for custody and immigration issues

What's Included for Children

  • After-school tutoring (K–8)
  • School supply and clothing assistance
  • Youth mentorship groups (ages 9–17)
  • Summer day camp scholarships

Eligibility

  • At least one child under 18 in the household
  • Pierce or Thurston County resident
  • Income at or below 250% of Federal Poverty Level
