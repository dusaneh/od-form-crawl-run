Long-Term Recovery

18-month intensive case management toward permanent housing and stable income.

Our Long-Term Recovery track pairs households with a dedicated coach who works with them for 18 months on housing stability, income growth, credit repair, and family goals. Participants graduate with a permanent housing plan, at least one household member employed at a living wage, and no active public debts.

Program Components

  • Weekly 90-minute coaching sessions
  • Monthly financial literacy and budgeting workshops
  • Credit repair support and match savings (up to $2,000 matched 2:1)
  • Employment placement with living-wage employer partners
  • Rental housing search and landlord mediation
  • Alumni network and post-graduation quarterly check-ins for 24 months

Eligibility

  • Actively unhoused or precariously housed at intake
  • Pierce or Thurston County resident
  • Income at or below 200% of Federal Poverty Level at intake
  • Ready and able to commit to weekly meetings for 18 months
  • Willing to consent to sharing information with employment partners
