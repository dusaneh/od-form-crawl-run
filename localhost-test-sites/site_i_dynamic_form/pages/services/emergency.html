Emergency Response

24-hour crisis intake for domestic violence survivors and families in imminent crisis.

24-hour crisis line: (253) 555-0999
Text line: Text HOPE to 741741

Domestic Violence Response

We operate a confidential 32-bed shelter for adults fleeing intimate partner violence, and their children. Address is confidential and shared only after completing a phone intake. Safety planning, protection order support, and legal advocacy are provided by our accredited DV advocates.

Homeless Family Diversion

For families with children who are facing imminent housing loss (72-hour eviction, eviction from a friend or family member's home), we can provide same-day funds for deposit, back rent, or a hotel stay while a permanent placement is secured.

Eligibility

  • DV shelter: any adult fleeing intimate partner violence, and their children
  • Diversion: family with at least one child under 18, imminent housing loss
  • No income or residency requirement for crisis response
