Individual Support

One-on-one case management for single adults 18 and over.

Our Individual Support track pairs each participant with a case manager who provides weekly appointments, benefits enrollment assistance (SNAP, Apple Health, SSDI, ORCA LIFT), mental health referrals, and transportation vouchers. Program duration is typically 6 to 9 months.

What's Included

  • Weekly one-hour case management appointments
  • Benefits enrollment and appeal support
  • Referrals to mental health and substance use treatment
  • Transportation vouchers (up to $80/month)
  • Emergency assistance for utilities, medications, and IDs (up to $500/year)

Eligibility

  • Age 18 or older
  • Household size of 1 (no dependents under 18 in the household)
  • Pierce or Thurston County resident
  • Income at or below 200% of Federal Poverty Level
